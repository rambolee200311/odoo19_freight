Freight Management Features

Advanced Dashboard

The Dashboard displays statics, shipment details, statuses, monthly shipments, move types, and billing graphs.

Quotation Request From Website

Shipment quotation requests submitted via the website for pricing and logistics inquiries.

Booking Reports

Booking reports include Bill of Lading, CMR, AWB, Shipment Instructions, Details, and Labels.

Ocean: LCL & FCL

Ocean shipments available in Less than Container Load (LCL) and Full Container Load (FCL) options.

Shipment Type: Direct, House & Master

Shipment types include Direct, House, and Master arrangements for various logistical needs.

Shipment Location Type

Shipment Location Type categorizes shipment destinations based on their characteristics and requirements.

Shipment Packages: Box, Pallet & Container

Shipment packages encompass boxes, pallets, and containers for diverse shipping needs.

Shipment Items

Shipment items refer to the goods or products being transported in a shipment.

Shipment Status Tracking

Shipment status tracking monitors the progress and location of shipped goods.

Dangerous Goods

Shipment of dangerous goods involves handling and transporting hazardous or regulated materials.

Shipment Bills & Invoices

Management of shipment bills and invoices ensures accurate financial transactions.

Website Tracking

Tracking shipments via the website provides real-time updates on their location.

Ocean Report: Bill of Lading Report

Ocean report detailing the Bill of Lading documentation for shipping transactions.

Land Report: CMR Report

Land report providing details on the CMR (Convention on the Contract for the International Carriage of Goods by Road).

Email Notifications

Email notifications alert recipients about shipment updates, status changes, or important information.

Vehicle: Airline, Road line & Vessels

Shipment vehicles include airlines, road lines, and vessels for various transportation needs.

Freight Quotation

Freight shipment quotation provides pricing estimates for transporting goods.

Freight Booking

Freight shipment booking involves reserving space on transportation carriers for cargo.

Shipment: Air, Ocean, & Land

Shipment services offered for air, ocean, and land transportation modes.

Land: FTL & LTL

Land transportation options include Full Truck Load (FTL) and Less Than Truck Load (LTL) services.

Shipment Notify Party

The Notify Party is informed of shipment details and serves as a contact point.

Shipment Dynamic Status

Dynamic status tracking provides real-time updates on shipment progress and location.

Shipment Weight Calculation

Weight calculation determines the total weight of the items in a shipment.

Custom Department

The customs department oversees and manages customs processes related to incoming and outgoing shipments.

Shipment Services

Shipment services encompass logistics, transportation, and handling of goods for delivery.

Shipment Insurance

Shipment insurance protects against loss or damage during the transportation process.

Shipment Accountancy

Shipment accountancy manages financial aspects such as billing, invoicing, and transaction records.

Portal Shipment & Booking Quotation

The portal facilitates shipment and booking by providing quotations for transportation services.

Air Report: Airway Bill (AWB)

Air report detailing Airway Bill (AWB) documentation for air cargo shipments.

Other Shipment Reports

Reports include Bill of Lading, CMR, Airway Bill (AWB), Shipment Instructions, Details, and Label Reports.

Port & Locations

Shipment ports and locations denote the points of departure, arrival, or transfer.

Tracking Templates

Shipment tracking templates standardize the format for monitoring and updating shipment statuses.
